Privacy & important information

AquaProfit is a free manufacturing cost-estimation and raw-material enquiry service. Calculations and saved packs are stored in your browser; they are not automatically sent to us. When you submit a quotation request, the information you provide is sent to Formspree for delivery to our team and a copy of the enquiry may be saved in this browser. We use enquiry details to respond to your request. Do not enter sensitive financial or personal information in the additional instructions.

Calculator outputs are estimates based on user-entered assumptions, not accounting, GST or legal advice. Prices, stock, delivery and specifications are confirmed separately when quoted. For privacy or enquiry questions contact contact@amritpani.in.
