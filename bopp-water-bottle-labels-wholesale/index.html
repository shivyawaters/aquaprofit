What to share when buying BOPP labels

  • Bottle size and label dimensions: share the actual label width and height, not only bottle volume.
  • Film type and thickness: confirm the suitable BOPP grade and micron specification for your labelling machine.
  • Printing: provide artwork, number of colours, finish and any barcode requirements.
  • Quantity: request a price for your expected order quantity and repeat monthly consumption.
  • Delivery: provide destination city or PIN code to compare landed costs.
  • Roll configuration: confirm roll direction, core size and labels per roll where applicable.

How to compare supplier quotes

Compare the delivered cost per usable label, not only the quoted rate. Check setup or cylinder charges, minimum order quantities, printing wastage, taxes, freight, lead time and replacement terms. Ask for a production sample to confirm print and machine compatibility.

Calculate label cost per water case

Multiply the label price per bottle by the number of bottles in each case. For example, at ₹0.16 per label, a 24-bottle case consumes ₹3.84 of labels before wastage. At 3% additional consumption, the estimated label material allowance is approximately ₹3.96 per case. This is an illustration, not a current supplier quote.
