1 Litre Water Bottle Manufacturing Cost per Case

A 1 litre water case often contains 12 bottles, so packaging consumption must be calculated for that pack count rather than copied from 24-bottle SKUs. Include the actual PET preform weight, cap, label, shrink, freight and factory overhead.

Worked example: 1-litre bottle, 12 bottles per case

Illustrative example: 12 bottles per case; preform 15.5g; PET ₹149/kg; cap ₹0.29; label ₹0.16; shrink ₹5 per case; wastage 3%; freight ₹7 per case; ex-GST selling price ₹95. PET costs ₹2.3095 per bottle. Materials before wastage total about ₹38.11 per case; with 3% wastage and ₹7 freight, variable cost is about ₹46.26. Contribution is about ₹48.74 before fixed expenses. Hypothetical example only.

Important: Example values are educational assumptions, not supplier offers, recommended selling prices or independently verified market rates.

Calculation method

  1. PET per bottle: preform grams × PET price per kg ÷ 1,000.
  2. Material per case: (PET + cap + label per bottle) × bottles per case + shrink per case.
  3. Variable cost: material per case × (1 + wastage %) + freight and other variable charges.
  4. Contribution: selling price excluding output GST − variable cost per case.
  5. Operating profit: total contribution − allocated monthly factory overhead. Consider input tax credit and GST separately.

For multiple SKUs, allocate factory overhead across the production mix using a consistent method such as machine hours, units or cases. The free calculator's single-SKU break-even assumes the entered fixed expenses belong to that SKU.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate manufacturing cost per case?

Multiply per-bottle PET, cap and label costs by the number of bottles in a case (12 in this example), then add shrink film, wastage and per-case freight. Allocate monthly factory expenses using actual total production.

Is the example a current supplier price?

No. It uses illustrative assumptions only. Request an actual quotation for materials and verify applicable taxes and freight.

How do I calculate profit per case?

Subtract variable cost per case from the selling price excluding GST to get contribution. Subtract appropriately allocated fixed factory costs to estimate operating profit. Loan EMI principal is a cash-flow item, not automatically an operating expense.
