About AquaProfit

AquaProfit is a free online cost-estimation and raw-material enquiry tool designed for packaged drinking water manufacturers in India.

Calculate production costsRequest a material quotation

What can manufacturers do?

Estimate manufacturing costs per bottle and per case for common pack sizes. Enter PET preform weight and price, cap and label costs, shrink-film consumption, wastage, freight, monthly factory expenses and selling price to estimate contribution and break-even.

Request quotations for PET preforms, bottle caps, BOPP labels, sticker labels, LDPE shrink film and corrugated boxes. If technical specifications are unknown, they may be left blank while requesting recommendations.

Who is AquaProfit for?

Packaged drinking water plant owners, production managers, procurement teams, distributors evaluating factory economics and businesses planning to start a water bottling unit in India.

Are prices live or guaranteed?

No. Calculations are estimates based on inputs entered by the user. AquaProfit facilitates raw-material enquiries; supplier quotations, specifications, taxes, availability and delivery terms require separate confirmation. The calculator is not professional tax, legal or accounting advice.
