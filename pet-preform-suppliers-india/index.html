PET preforms for packaged drinking water manufacturers

PET preform weight and resin price are major drivers of bottle cost. When requesting a quote, share your required weight in grams if known, quantity in kilograms, delivery city and intended bottle size. If you do not know the exact weight, describe your bottle and ask for a compatible recommendation. Compatibility must be checked against your mould and closure system before ordering.

What manufacturers should know

Compare supplier quotations on resin grade, consistency, neck compatibility, actual weight, wastage, GST, freight, lead time and payment terms. The lowest price per kilogram is not necessarily the lowest finished-bottle cost.
