Privacy & important information

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Calculations and saved packs

Calculator inputs and saved packs remain in your browser and are not automatically sent as enquiries. Saved packs use browser storage and can be removed by clearing this site’s browser data. Export them before clearing storage if you want to retain a copy.

Material enquiries

When you send an enquiry, the details shown in the review are sent through Formspree to the AquaProfit team. This includes your contact details, requirement and any calculator context you choose to share. Your current material price is included only if you select that option.

We use enquiry information to respond and coordinate quotations. Contact aquaprofitstore@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion or retention of your enquiry. This page does not promise a fixed retention period or automatic deletion from third-party systems.

Visits and external services

The website has a visit counter. Visits are not necessarily unique people. News feeds and linked third-party websites may receive standard connection information when requested or opened.

Estimates and sourcing

Calculations depend on your assumptions and are not professional accounting or tax advice. Break-even assumes the cases are sold at the entered price and contribution. EMI is a cash-flow target. GST differences are not guaranteed refunds. Supplier quotations, samples, compatibility, availability, delivery and payment terms must be confirmed before ordering.
